## Owner decisions needed
1. **Retired signing key (S1).** Treat it as compromised. Options (each changes signing, so not done here): remove its capabilities in the lineage (`apksigner lineage --set-installed-data false --set-rollback false` on the old signer), force updates with `MIN_BUILD`, raise `minSdk` to 28 (Android 8 devices only verify with the old key). Rewriting history does not undo the leak.
2. Enable Dependabot alerts and secret scanning in the repository settings (they are off).
3. ROM helper (S4/S5): enforcing SELinux policy and signature check, or document the risk.
