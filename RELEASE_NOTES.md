## What's new
- "Close background apps" in game mode now also closes the phone's own apps that have an icon (camera, calendar, gallery, notes and similar) and keeps them closed while you play. Their settings are not changed: they only come back if you open them. The Play Store, camera, weather and similar apps that the system used to reopen right away are now kept closed too, and everything goes back as it was when you leave the game. The clock and Settings are closed but not blocked, so alarms always ring. Calls, the keyboard, the launcher, the system security app and music that is playing are never touched. An app that opens anyway (for example to receive a message) is closed again within about 10 seconds. A phone app that the system itself restarts again and again (some keep the camera or the store ready) is left alone after a few tries, since closing it in a loop would only use more battery.
- Game mode keeps a short log of when each session starts and ends, and why, to help explain a session that ends on its own.
- Game mode no longer ends when you open Monitor to check your stats in the middle of a game, and it starts again reliably when you go back to the game (some changes of the app on screen, like closing a floating window, were missed).
