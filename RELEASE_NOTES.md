## What's new
- "Close background apps" in game mode now closes more: besides your installed apps, the phone's own apps with an icon (camera, calendar, gallery, weather, the Play Store, Settings, the clock and similar). While you play they are kept closed, and an app that opens anyway (for example to receive a message) is closed again within about 10 seconds. Everything goes back as it was when you leave the game.
- Never touched: calls, the keyboard, the launcher, the system security app, music that is playing, VPNs and accessibility services. Alarms always ring.
- A phone app that the system itself restarts again and again is left alone after a few tries, since closing it in a loop would only use more battery.
- The app you have on screen is never closed, so you can open a chat during a game.
- Game mode no longer ends when you open Monitor to check your stats in the middle of a game, and it starts again reliably when you go back to the game.
- Game mode keeps a short log of when each session starts and ends, and why.
