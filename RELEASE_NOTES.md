## What's new
- "Close background apps" in game mode now also closes the phone's own apps that have an icon (camera, calendar, gallery, notes and similar) and keeps them closed while you play. Their settings are not changed: they only come back if you open them. The clock, Settings and the Play Store are closed too (alarms still ring and downloads keep going). Calls, the keyboard, the launcher, the system security app and music that is playing are never touched. Phone apps that the system reopens by itself on purpose are left alone after a couple of tries, since closing them in a loop would only use more battery.
- Game mode keeps a short log of when each session starts and ends, and why, to help explain a session that ends on its own.
- Game mode no longer ends when you open Monitor to check your stats in the middle of a game, and it starts again reliably when you go back to the game (some changes of the app on screen, like closing a floating window, were missed).
